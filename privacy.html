Family Hub — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

The one-sentence version: Family Hub runs without servers. Your data lives in your own Google Drive, protected by your own Google account. We never see it, store it, or sell it — because there is nowhere for it to go.

What Family Hub is

Family Hub is a shared calendar, notes, votes, chores and shopping list app for families. Each family member signs in with their own Google account, and the family's data is stored as plain files in a shared Google Drive folder that the family themselves own.

What we access and why

When you sign in, you grant these Google permissions:

  • View and edit events on your calendars (calendar.readonly, calendar.events) — so Family Hub can show your schedule and add events you create in the app.
  • See, edit, create, and delete files in your Google Drive that Family Hub touches (drive.file) — this is limited to the Family Hub folder itself. The app cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • See your name, email, and profile picture — to identify you to your family in the app.

We request nothing else. Your contacts, mail, photos, location, and the rest of your Drive are never accessed.

What we collect

Nothing. Family Hub has no servers, no databases, no analytics, and no advertising. Your information never leaves your devices and your family's Drive folder.

Where data lives and who controls it

All family data — notes, votes, chores, shopping lists, and configuration — lives in a shared Google Drive folder created by the person who sets up the family. You can view, export, or delete any of it at any time from Google Drive itself. Deleting the folder removes the family's data. Revoking access in your Google Account settings removes the app's access.

Updates and security

Sign-in credentials are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted anywhere except to Google for authentication. Updates never remove your sign-in.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/caradeen333-ux/family-hub.

Family Hub is open-source software provided as-is. Google's use of your data is governed by Google's own privacy policy; Family Hub only uses the permissions above through Google's APIs, directly from your device.
